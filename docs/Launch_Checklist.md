# LAUNCH CHECKLIST — COMPLETE BEFORE ACCEPTING CUSTOMERS

## Business identity and compliance

- [ ] Choose a service name and check the name/domain is available; don't assume the placeholder name is free.
- [ ] Set your legal/trading name, genuine business contact email, UK business contact address and other required contact details.
- [ ] Complete the draft privacy and terms pages with actual legal details and correct cancellation and delivery terms (not blanket no-returns language).
- [ ] Decide if sole-trader structure is suitable; arrange bookkeeping and check HMRC reporting rules and VAT status as appropriate.
- [ ] Check business/home insurance and appropriate public/product liability cover.
- [ ] Assess UK product safety, testing, intended usage, model licensing, and product labelling and reject work beyond your validated capabilities.
- [ ] Establish how long you retain quote files, customer details and financial records; plan secure deletion and backup.
- [ ] Confirm data protection compliance, your hosting provider's terms and ICO requirements.

## Systems

- [ ] Put unique secrets into secure environment variables, never in source code.
- [ ] Host over HTTPS and persist both SQLite database and private file uploads.
- [ ] Test form validation, login, file download protection, backups and recovery, and deletion process.
- [ ] Set up working business email and a secure payment account separately.
- [ ] Check every quotation includes total price, delivery, timeframe, and relevant terms, and is sent manually.
- [ ] Set up correctly sized parcel packaging, postage provider and returns/complaint contact.
- [ ] Test one end-to-end enquiry using your own test model and a non-live payment or no payment.
- [ ] Remove the pre-launch banner only by setting PUBLIC_LAUNCH=1 after review.

## Commercial pilot

- [ ] Prepare sample prints and honest photographs of your own work.
- [ ] Set minimum order price and handling fee from cost/time calculations.
- [ ] Agree and record print time, material, machine wear, labour, fees, shipping and margin for each job.
- [ ] Measure number of enquiries, paid orders, revenue, contribution and **hands-on hours**.
- [ ] Re-evaluate after 30 days; don't buy a second printer until the first has repeatable profitable demand.

# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SINGLE PRINTER

**Request**: Check contact, file type, intended use, rights declaration and requested material/quantity. Reject dangerous, safety-critical, medical, weapon, obviously infringing, or otherwise unsuitable jobs.

**Validate**: Open in a trusted slicer, inspect orientation, manifold errors, dimensions (mm), wall thickness, supports and expected tolerance. Confirm nozzle diameter and material. If changes are needed, agree them in writing before payment.

**Quote**: Record grams, machine hours, materials, your active minutes, postage estimate, packaging, payment fees and failure allowance. Check physical print feasibility and capacity. Include total price (tax treatment as applicable), delivery charge, estimated dispatch and customer terms.

**Payment**: Create secure payment link in a payment provider dashboard. Don't handle card numbers yourself. Verify payment in that dashboard — a customer email or screenshot isn't proof of funds.

**Print**: Inspect printer and first layer; adhere to ventilation, operating and fire precautions. Monitor progress as required; keep print profile/settings traceable to order ID. Never claim specific strength or safety standards without testing.

**Quality**: Check quantity, dimensions against agreed tolerances, visual defects, layer adhesion and fit. Record failures and reprints. Photograph product for internal quality purposes with appropriate privacy practices.

**Ship**: Package for impact protection; weigh and measure parcel; select suitable delivery service and insurance, then communicate tracking. Record delivered/completed status.

**Close**: Record material, print time, handling time, direct costs, price, refunds and contribution. Store order records and customer designs securely according to your documented retention policy.
